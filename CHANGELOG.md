# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Air quality in the menu bar: PM2.5, PM10, VOC, NO₂, formaldehyde, temperature and humidity, rated like the MyDyson app.
- Control power, auto mode, fan speed, oscillation range, night mode, airflow direction and sleep timer.
- Heating on Hot+Cool and humidifying on Humidify+Cool models.
- Seven days of history with sparklines and charts.
- Notifications for bad air, used-up filters and device faults.
- Sign in to MyDyson once inside the app; everything after that runs over your local network.
- Several purifiers with a switcher, English and German, launch at login, optional Dock icon.
